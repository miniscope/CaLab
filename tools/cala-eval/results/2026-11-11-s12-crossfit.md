# S1.2 cross-fitted footprint statistics (`--crossfit parity-loo`) vs main

- Base: main 81f041c (E6 runs, `e6/runs/x1/base`). New: 0b44ca8 = this branch with `crossfit: ParityLoo`.
  Same recordings, `--batch 8 --budget 30000000 --slow-seed 1`, 600 px, 9600 frames, seeds 5-9.
- Pre-declared primary metric (plan S1.2): `fp_noise` on `oof` and `motion`; secondary P8, R8 non-inferior.

## Verdict

No win, so the default stays `Off` and `ParityLoo` is opt-in.

- **Primary metric.** `fp_noise` shows no effect on either claim arm (the CI covers 0). Main has only 1.0 (oof) and 5.4 (motion) noise FPs per run, so there is little for the change to remove.
- **Precision.** P8 is up on `motion` (+.016 ± .009, 5/0 seeds) and down on `halo` (−.012 ± .010, 0/5 seeds; `fp_partial` +4.4). It is flat on `static` and `oof`.
- **Recall.** R8 is within ±.01 on every arm, but its CIs (about ±.03) do not show non-inferiority at −.02.
- **Real data** (workshop, 18 000 frames, `--core-test report`). Textured components did not collapse:
  - texture-flagged cells rose from 28 to 42;
  - the median `r_split` of textured cells fell only from .90 to .79, and 7 of 41 are below .5 (target: half);
  - Minian agreement at r ≥ .5 fell from 511 to 504.
- **Interpretation (plan D4).** Even with the halves cross-fitted, the texture reproduces, so it is in the data and not a noise fixed point of the estimator. The cause is C (motion, shimmer or pixel set), and S1.5's placebo must include it.
