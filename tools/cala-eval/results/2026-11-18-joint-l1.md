# L1 trace step in the cluster replay (flag off by default)

18 Nov 2026. Branch `feature/cala-joint-pair-fit` (from main 6752419b, which has the cluster replay #422 + #424).
`SlowLoopConfig::joint_l1` (`qc_eval --joint-l1 <μ>`, default 0 = off), used with `--joint-update refine`.

## The problem

A wide cell next to a small one is left bitten: its footprint misses its tail under the small cell, and the small
cell's trace carries `λ c_wide`. That split (`a_wide − λ a_small`, `c_small + λ c_wide`) reconstructs the frames as
well as the truth, so neither the running statistics, nor the replay's plain non-negative alternation, nor the
held-out χ² gate can tell them apart. One-sided repairs (bite refill, trim, unmix; `2026-11-18-bite-refill.md` on
`fix/cala-wide-neighbour-tail`) move the leak from the earlier-born to the later-born cell instead of removing it.

## The change

The replay already fits both footprints and both traces of an overlapping cluster jointly on its stored frames.
Its trace step now takes an L1 weight:

`c_t = argmin_{c ≥ 0} ‖U v_t − A c‖² + 2 μ Σ_k ‖a_k‖ c_k` (whitened)

- `μ` is in trace-noise SDs: a soft threshold of `μ / ‖a_k‖` on member `k`'s trace, invariant to the footprints'
  scale.
- **Why it breaks the tie.** With unit-norm footprints the bitten split has a larger trace L1 than the truth, by
  `Σ c_wide ‖a_wide‖ (√(1 − ρ²) + ρ − 1) > 0` (`ρ` the footprints' overlap). The sparse split is the
  independent one.
- **Fallback.** When the threshold silences a member in either half of the stored frames (a dim member), that
  replay uses the plain trace step (otherwise the footprint fit is singular and the cluster drops to the per-cell
  path; see v2 below).
- Only the replay's internal trace step changes. The gate (paired held-out χ², unpenalised traces), the finish
  path and the published traces are untouched. Flag off is byte-identical to main.

Test `l1_trace_step_heals_the_leak_fixed_point` (`discover/cluster.rs`): a wide cell (1.4x) 0.5 D from a small one,
started at the fixed point. Plain replay stays there (leak 0.54); `μ = 2` brings the tail back (0.96 of truth) and the
leak to 0.03, with no reverse leak; started at the truth it stays there. An ignored sweep (`bitten_pair_sweep`)
gives the toy's dose response.

## Measured and not adopted

- **More alternations per replay** (`--joint-iters 3`, μ 2): no better than one (pairs leak > 0.2: 3).
- **Carry** (`--joint-carry`, μ 2): worse (p90 0.098, bitten 6), the over-fill seen in Stage 2.
- **μ = 5**: no better than 3 on the pairs (leak > 0.2: 3, p90 0.074).
