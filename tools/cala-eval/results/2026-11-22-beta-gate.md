# Beta gate: look-back Discover + Final settle against v2.20.0's Discover + Refine (22 Nov 2026)

The plan and pass lines were committed and pushed before any measured run:
[2026-11-22-beta-gate-plan.md](2026-11-22-beta-gate-plan.md), commit `b6d544fb`. Anything added after the runs is
marked **(post hoc)**.

- **Old path:** v2.20.0 (tag `898b6e73`, its own worktree and WASM build). Discover, then one Refine.
- **New path:** `main` `6a579e05` + this branch's driver change, opened with `?lookback`. Discover with look-back, then
  the Final settle. Background settle off, no pacing (Max).

## Short answer

**NO-GO by the rule written in advance.** The new path does not reproduce the old path's results closely enough.

1. **Footprints come out different, not noisier.**
   - On all three recordings the new path's footprints agree less with the old path's than old runs agree with each
     other. The median cosine is 0.003–0.008 lower (line: 0.0013–0.002).
   - Two new runs agree with each other as well as two old runs do (0.9917 against 0.9920; 0.9913 against 0.9906;
     0.9813 against 0.9790). So the new path is just as repeatable. It converges to a different answer.
2. **The new footprints are larger.**
   - After the Final settle the median footprint has 975–1,190 pixels. After Refine it has 665–785, so the new
     footprints are about 33–75 % larger (`10_43_21` +33 %, `14_57_02` +53 %, workshop +75 %).
   - The faint part (energy below 10 % of peak) rises from 0.1–0.5 % to 1.0–1.2 %.
   - The cores differ too: cut to pixels at or above 10 % of peak, the gap stays the same size.
   - Most of the growth happens in the Final settle. The settle adds 280–480 pixels per footprint; Refine adds 115–175.
3. **The new path loses cells on the busier recordings.**
   - Missing cells exceed the old path's own run-to-run miss by 2.3, 4.0 and 6.9 points (sa, sb, ws). The line fails
     on `14_57_02` and the workshop.
   - Counts: workshop 745 against 835 (−11 %), `14_57_02` 280 against 295 (−5 %), `10_43_21` 322 against 330 (−2 %).
   - Of the cells all three old runs find, each new run has 90–95 %. The old runs' own leave-one-out figure is 92–95 %.
4. **It is 12–17 % slower and uses 0.2–0.5 GB more memory.**
   - Time: +17 s, +24 s and +35 s. The line, set from the old path's spread, is 7–27 s, so time fails everywhere.
   - Memory passes on two recordings and fails on `14_57_02` (+0.45 GB against a 0.20 GB line). All runs stay under
     3.2 GB.
5. **No errors.** Every run finished, no page errors, every look-back check passed.
6. **The cause is the look-back + Final settle path, not other code changes since v2.20.0 (post hoc control).**
   - `main` with `?lookback` off, one run per recording, matches v2.20.0 within the old path's own spread.
   - Median cosine against the old runs: 0.9915, 0.9900 and 0.9772. Old against old: 0.9920, 0.9906 and 0.9790.
   - Footprint size is the same as v2.20.0, and time is 117–255 s.

"Different from the old path" is not the same as "worse". Real data has no ground truth. The sims studies (#439, #443)
found the settle level with look-back + Refine against ground truth. That was in qc_eval, whose visit model fails its
calibration at full speed (#456, #461). The PI has said truncated footprints leave light behind, so larger is not
wrong in itself. Whether 40–75 % larger footprints are better or worse cannot be decided from these runs. See "What
would need fixing".

**Readings.**

1. **The difference is the look-back path.** `main` without look-back reproduces v2.20.0. The code changes since the
   tag (#426, #458, #460 and the rest) do not move the plain path.
2. **The new path is as repeatable as the old.** Its gap to the old path is a systematic shift, not extra noise.
3. **The footprints grow in two places.**
   - Look-back Discover ends with footprints about 15–40 % larger than plain Discover.
   - The Final settle then adds 40–68 %, against Refine's 18–36 %.
   - The settle also moves most cells by more than their noise. Its own readout: 43–46 % of cells moved more than
     twice their split-half noise on Samara, 79 % on the workshop, with a median change 1.8–3.1× the noise.
4. **The differences are in the cores too, not only in a faint skirt.** Cut both footprints to pixels at or above
   10 % of their peak, and the old-new gap stays the same.
5. **The lost cells come from Discover.** The settle keeps the cell set fixed. (One workshop run ended with one cell
   more than it began; that was not investigated.)
   - The new path's Discover drops 2–8× more frames for the slow loop than the old path's. The slow worker also serves
     the look-back answers.
   - The cluster replay (on in look-back Discover) is the other known cause (#443: workshop 818 cells with replay off
     against 750 with it on).

## Sims anchor

Not rerun (plan). qc_eval cannot run the v2.20.0 code, and its visit model fails calibration at full speed. The
existing ground-truth evidence:

- #439 and #443: settle = look-back + Refine on 14 sims;
- #430: footprint r against truth level with Discover + Refine on `main` at the time.

That evidence says the settle is not worse against truth in the model. It does not cover the footprint growth seen
here. The sims' footprint metric would need a size or support check to tell.

## Verdict: NO-GO

By the rule written in advance:

- A1 fails on all three recordings;
- A2 fails on two;
- B1 fails on two.

Time and memory would only make it a conditional go. The default should not switch, and Refine should not be removed,
on this evidence.

## What would need fixing before a rerun

1. **Find out why the settle grows footprints so much more than Refine, and decide which size is right.**
   - Compare the settle and Refine on the same look-back Discover library. The app's `--lookback --refine` already
     does this; the remaining step is to measure the footprint sizes.
   - Run the sims with a footprint-support metric (pixels against the true footprint's support, and the light left
     outside it), so "larger" can be scored as better or worse against truth.
   - Candidate causes, not tested here:
     - the settle's re-read frames use the end-of-Discover background and noise state, not each frame's own;
     - the settle reads 64-frame blocks spread over the recording, not the recording in order;
     - the cluster replay is on in the settle;
     - the held-out gate passes more support growth.
2. **Recover the lost cells on dense recordings.** This is the cell loss in look-back Discover (workshop −11 %): fewer
   frames dropped for the slow loop (spare-compute idea #1, moving the per-frame stats update off the slow worker), and
   the replay's cost in Discover (#443).
3. **Then rerun this gate** with the same script and lines, on a quiet machine. Time is likely to stay 10–20 % slower;
   that part is a PI trade-off, not a quality question.

If the PI judges the larger footprints to be the better answer (for example, from sims with a support metric, or from
inspecting the larger cells), A1 and A2 measure a difference he accepts. The decision would then rest on the cell loss
(B1) and the time cost.
