# S2.4 M0: how much the spatial scales matter, and whether the offline estimators work (12 Nov 2026)

M0 of S2.4 (data-driven spatial scales) only measures. It changes no default. This PR adds:

- `qc_eval` flags `--cell-diameter`, `--psf-sigma`, `--flatten-sigma`, `--tile-px`, `--margin-px` and `--bin K`
  (`real` only: mean of each K x K block, rounded to u8).
- `SlowLoopConfig::psf_sigma_px`, defaulting to the old constant `PSF_SIGMA_PX` (1.5 px).
- `tools/cala-eval/fp_scales.py` (D1 and P1 on a run's footprints), `probe_scale.py` (an offline D0 prototype),
  the ring edge statistic in `gt_scales.py`, and `GT_SCALES` and `SCALE_ARMS` in `arms.py`.

**Identity.** With none of the new flags, the binary gives byte-identical outputs to main `59986a4` on
`v3_motion_s5` (9600 frames, `--support-rule basin-skirt`). Every file matches except wall-time columns
(`cost.txt`, the wall column of `passes.tsv`, `solve_s` of `flat_field_updates.tsv`). Passing the defaults
explicitly (`--cell-diameter 10 --psf-sigma 1.5 --flatten-sigma 20 --tile-px 64 --margin-px 24`, 2400 frames)
is also identical to main.

**Setup.** Every run used `--support-rule basin-skirt --batch 8 --budget 30000000 --slow-seed 1`. The rule is
passed explicitly so that these results do not depend on which support rule is the default on main. Sims are the
existing v3 recordings with minisim 1.1.0 flags (`~/Projects/cala-data/sims-v3`, 600 px, 480 s). No new sims
were generated: `size6`, `v3` and `size16` are the 6 / 10 / 16 px arms the plan asked for, at the real
recording's edge blur. Real runs used the workshop recording (18 000 frames, `--motion`). P8 and R8 are
precision and recall at the end of the run against the detectable ground truth (r >= 0.7, Hungarian matching).

## 1. Sensitivity of P8 and R8 to each scale (question 1)

One scale moves at a time, on static sims, seeds 5-7. The `d` arms pin the flatten σ at 20 so that only the
detection-side scales move. `d` is the paired difference against `base` (`d` = 10, PSF 1.5, flatten 20), with
a 95 % t-CI over seeds. Arms with n = 2 have wide CIs (t = 12.7).

**Why static.** The motion arms show a global offset between cala's registered frame and the ground-truth frame.
qc_eval's matching does not compensate it (section 6). On 6 px cells that offset alone drops P8/R8 to
0.16/0.17. Static runs avoid it. The few motion runs that were made agree with the static ones (section 6).

**Reading.**

- **Diameter.** The cost is lopsided:
  - Too small is expensive. `d` = 6 on 16 px cells finds 312 cells against 724 (R8 -0.47). On 10 px cells it
    costs 0.056 R8.
  - Too large is cheap. 1.6x (v3 at 16) costs 0.027 R8 and 0.009 P8; 2.7x (size6 at 16) costs 0.036 R8.
  - The matched value is not always the best one. On 6 px cells, `d` = 10 beats `d` = 6 (P8 +0.033, R8 +0.018).
    The seed disk (0.75 d) and the detection blur (d/4) seem to need some room above the cell.
  - So an estimator needs a lower margin, not precision. It must not read low by more than about 1.4x. Reading
    high by up to 1.6x costs at most about 0.03 R8.
  - On real data `d` = 16 keeps the cell count and the r ≥ .5 Minian agreement (526 against 531 and 534), loses
    17 at r ≥ .7, and has lower centroid agreement than the run-to-run reference (387 against 464).
- **PSF σ.** Over 0.75-3 px every difference is inside its CI and at most 0.015 in size. Real data also stays
  within run-to-run spread (Minian 527 / 414). P1 accuracy is therefore not on the critical path.
- **Flatten σ.**
  - Too narrow hurts big cells. At σ = 10 (0.63 d on size16) P8 falls by 0.107 ± 0.039. On v3 (1 d) it falls by
    0.035 ± 0.028.
  - On sims, a broad flattener is free or better: σ = 40 and 80 give P8 +0.047 ± 0.029 and +0.049 ± 0.022 on
    size16.
  - On the real recording it is not free. σ = 40 is at the edge of the run-to-run spread (Minian 522 against
    531 and 534). σ = 80 loses 30 Minian matches and about 40 cells, and the median `ho_marginal_z` drops from
    5.4 to 2.6. The real recording has glow structure between 20 and 80 px that the sims lack.
  - Rule supported by the data: σ_b ≈ 2 d̂, the current relation, with `d̂` the estimate. Broader than about
    4 d̂ costs on real data. Narrower than about 1 d̂ costs on large cells.

## 2. The scale probe D0 on 400 frames (question 2)

`probe_scale.py` implements section 2.2:

1. Rigid phase-correlation registration of the first 400 frames.
2. Subtraction of the per-pixel temporal median, then whitening by the per-pixel noise (MAD of frame differences).
3. Scale-normalised LoG on a √2 ladder from 0.6 px to the FOV's inscribed radius / 18.
4. Local maxima over (y, x, σ) above `event_z` = 4.
5. Sites kept only if they vote in at least `event_frames` = 3 frames.
6. Conversion of σ* to `d` with the blurred-disk relation.

Nothing is tuned; every constant is an existing cala setting or measured geometry.

| recordings (400 frames)         | GT `d`, px | `d0` (sites), px, seeds 5-9 | `d0` / GT | pass (within 1.4x) |
| ------------------------------- | ---------- | --------------------------- | --------- | ------------------ |
| v3 static                       | 10.1       | 10.7, 8.8, 9.4, 9.7, 7.6    | .75-1.06  | 5 / 5              |
| v3 motion                       | 10.1       | 9.4, 6.9, 9.1, 7.0, 7.6     | .68-.93   | 3 / 5              |
| size6 static                    | 6.1        | 4.6, 6.9, 6.9, 7.8, 6.6     | .76-1.29  | 5 / 5              |
| size6 motion                    | 6.1        | 6.9, 4.3, 7.5, 7.4, 5.6     | .71-1.23  | 4 / 5              |
| size16 static                   | 15.8       | 5.2, 9.1, 7.0, 8.9, 7.6     | .33-.57   | 0 / 5              |
| size16 motion                   | 15.8       | 5.2, 6.9, 3.4, 9.3, 6.9     | .21-.59   | 0 / 5              |
| v2 static / motion s5 (14.4 px) | 14.5       | 8.3 / 8.1                   | .57 / .56 | 0 / 2              |
| workshop                        | (D1 9.97)  | 9.4                         | .94       | yes                |
| workshop 2x binned              | (D1 5.64)  | 4.1                         | .73       | yes                |

- It fails exactly where it matters. Section 1 shows that reading low is the expensive error, and the probe
  reads 0.2-0.6x on large cells.
- Why it fails: dark maxima (the same search on the negated frames) are about as frequent as bright ones in
  every band (`dark_per_band` in the JSON). Most votes are fluctuations of neuropil, motion residual and
  neighbouring cells, not single-cell events. A large soma is also often several overlapping cells, which the
  probe sees as smaller blobs.
- Using the dark maxima as a null to keep only significant bands removed nearly all votes, so it is reported
  but not used.
- An image-based variant (scale-space blobs on the peak-to-noise or standard-deviation image of the 400 frames)
  read 2.5-6 px on all arms, which is worse.

PSF and flatten arms do not move D1 (within ±0.01 of base) or the ring median.

**D1.**

- It recovers the GT statistic to -7 % (10 and 16 px) / +2 % (6 px), well inside the ±20 % acceptance.
- It is unbiased when the assumed `d` is too small, which is the self-correction the plan predicted.
- It is inflated when the assumed `d` is too large: +15 % at 2.7x too large (size6 at 16), +11 % on real data at
  1.6x, +17 % on the 2x binned recording at 2x. The plan expected the opposite here ("the half-max area of the
  cells found is unaffected").
- The fixed point still converges from above. Each step moves the applied `d` towards `d̂`, which lies far
  inside the 1.4x margin, so a start that is too large is self-correcting. A start that is too small is also
  self-correcting in D1, but it costs recall while it lasts (section 1).
- On real data D1 agrees with itself: 9.97 (95 % CI 9.84-10.22) and 9.77 (9.57-9.97) for seeds 1 and 2, and
  10.09-10.12 under the PSF and flatten arms.
- On the 2x binned recording with given scales it reads 5.64 binned px. That is 1.13x of half the
  full-resolution value, inside the ±20 % acceptance of section 6.4.

**P1.**

- The per-cell ring σ is close to the GT ring σ on matched cells: 0.98-1.0 on 10 and 16 px cells, 0.87 on 6 px.
- The population bound fails. `min_i(s_i + z SE_i)` is 0.5-1.1 px against a GT ring σ of 1.5-1.6 and a GT q10
  of about 1.3. The premise "every bias but noise is upward" does not hold for cala's footprints. Basin cuts and
  the flattener inversion make some edges steeper than the optics (`min s` is 0.07-0.3 px).
- The SE here is a proxy (scatter within each ring), so the bound's z term is not the measured SE of PR 3.
  The minima themselves are already far below the truth, however, so a correct SE would not rescue the minimum.
- On real data the ring median is 2.13 px, wider than on any sim (1.3-1.6). Real footprints are blurrier than
  v3, or carry more sub-cell structure.
- Since σ_psf has no measurable effect on P8/R8 (section 1), P1 can wait. If it is wanted, use a per-cell
  median with an order-statistic CI, not a minimum.
- The vessel-edge cross-check P2 was not run (no tissue image is dumped). It is not needed while σ_psf does
  not matter.

## 5. The two open choices, and what changes in the plan

1. **D0 (section 2.2 / 8).** The probe fails on large cells, and reading low is the expensive direction. Do not
   build PR 4 as planned. Instead (contingency A, revised under the PI's "nothing is frozen"):
   - Start detection at a deliberately large `d0`.
   - Let D1 pull it down at commit boundaries. D1 is unbiased from below and at most +15 % from 2.7x above, so
     the fixed point converges.
   - Let the flatten σ follow at 2 d̂ under the transition rule of PR 2.
   - Pass 2 reprocesses with the final values.
2. **No evidence at the start (section 2.4).** The noise-limited broadest flattener is unnecessary and harmful
   on real data (σ 80 loses about 30 Minian matches). The data show that a starting `d0` is needed and that it
   should err high. Question 2 to the PI becomes: **which declared starting diameter?** The evidence favours a
   value at the large end of plausible somata (about 1.6x the expected one costs ≤ 0.03 R8 while D1 settles).
   A stored per-sensor value, where one exists, replaces it.
3. **Transition rule (PI revision).** It could not be measured with this harness, because a mid-run scale
   change needs PR 2's `set_scales`. The sensitivity numbers above bound what is at stake: on 16 px cells a
   flatten σ that is too narrow costs 0.1 P8. It is the first measurement for PR 2 (reset against reweight of
   the reservoir and `FpStats` on a flatten change).

## 6. Side findings

- **Motion arms: ground-truth frame offset.** qc_eval's GT matching assumes cala's registered frame equals the
  sim's tissue frame. On motion sims the template sits off by a few pixels:
  - `size6_motion_s5`: (3, 1) px; the summed-footprint image r against GT is .615 unshifted and .908 shifted.
  - `v3_motion_s5`: (1, -1) px; .915 against .944.
  - `size6_static_s5`: (0, 0).

  With 6 px cells this alone drops P8/R8 to 0.16/0.17, and the same holds under the `rings` support rule. The
  10 and 16 px motion arms lose less. Motion-arm P8/R8 partly measure this offset. This should be fixed in
  qc_eval (estimate the template-to-tissue shift, or match with a shift search) before the S2.4 acceptance
  matrix uses motion arms. Motion runs that were made agree with the static ones: v3_motion `d` 6 / 16 gives
  R8 -0.062 / -0.016 at n = 1, and size16_motion `d` 6 gives R8 -0.51.

- **Matched `d` is not optimal on 6 px cells** (section 1). `given` arms in the acceptance matrix (section 6.3)
  should not be read as an upper bound.

## 7. Cost, and what was cut

The run was 85 qc_eval runs (72 sim, 8 real, 3 identity, 2 diagnostic) at ≤ 2 of this task's processes at once
and ≤ 4 qc_eval processes machine-wide. Sim runs took 3-6.5 min and ≤ 1.4 GB, real runs 6-16 min and ≤ 1.4 GB.
The probe was 32 recordings of 400 frames each.

Cut, by priority:

- the `d` 14 arm everywhere;
- seed 7 of the PSF and flatten 10 arms;
- seeds 8 and 9 of everything;
- the real `d` 6, PSF 0.75, flatten 10 and `d` 14 runs;
- after the motion-offset finding, the remaining motion runs, which were replaced by static ones.
