# QC peak SNR: low because of the window, not the noise estimate

13 Nov 2026. Branch `fix/qc-snr`.

## The problem

The PI saw it on real data after Refine: footprints with clean traces and clear transients fail QC on a low peak SNR, and the SNR does not follow how clean the traces look. This note looks for the cause, compares SNR definitions against ground truth, and fixes the app path.

## Verdict

1. **The window is the cause.** The app shows Discover's QC: each cell's last 60 s of trace (`window_frames` 1200 at 20 Hz), from the last snapshot before Discover ends. Refine and Extract run no QC monitor (`qc: false`), so that snapshot stays on screen and goes into the export.
   - A clean cell that is quiet in the last minute reads low.
   - On real data, the same statistic over the whole session gets 70 of 71 failing cells to pass, and none goes the other way.
   - The whole-session statistic ranks cells against the reference far better than the window does. Against Minian agreement: AUC .64 → .80, and ρ with the Minian trace r .35 → .75. Against GT on sims: AUC .85–.89 → .91–.95, and ρ with GT SNR .68–.69 → .89–.91.
2. **Clipping bias is refuted.**
   - The σ that skips zero pairs is _smaller_ than the symmetric σ of the signed traces, not larger. On median it is 0.86–0.89× on sims and 0.87× on real data.
   - The clip truncates the noise of half-clipped pairs.
   - So the clipped σ makes the SNR read a little high. It does not explain the low values.
   - The symmetric σ (signed traces, or the lab's MAD over all pairs computed on signed traces) ranks cells no better than the current σ over the same span. Its AUC is within ±.005 and its ρ is .01–.05 lower.
3. **A multi-session bug** (found by the coordinator on the PI's Joint run).
   - Every session's export carried the same QC: the end of the last session.
4. **The fix** (this PR).
   - **QC after Extract.** After Extract, the app recomputes the trace statistics on the extracted traces, per session span, with the monitor's own function.
     - The scope is `'window'` (default: the last 60 s of the span, the monitor's definition) or `'session'` (the whole span).
     - The default fixes the stale and shared numbers but keeps the definition.
     - **Proposal: make `'session'` the default.** It is the span CaLab's shipped SNR convention reads (`crates/solver/src/snr.rs`, whole trace) and the best definition measured here.
   - **QC durations follow the recording's frame rate.** Every QC duration is now seconds × the recording's fps (`QcConfig::for_fps`). At 20 Hz the defaults are identical; this is asserted in a test.

**The threshold.**

- At 5, every whole-session definition passes nearly every cell, false positives included: 52/52 on motion, 30/31 on static.

## Real data

The recording is the workshop CA1 recording: 600×600, 18000 frames, 20 Hz (timestamps and metaData.json). Flags: `--motion --frames 18000 --batch 8 --budget 30000000 --via-jobs --slow-seed 1 --dump`. The run has 815 cells.

There is no GT. The reference is Minian, which is not ground truth (missing and wrong cells), so these are agreement numbers:

- Minian footprint r ≥ .5 is used for the AUC; 585 of 815 cells.
- ρ is taken with the correlation between cala's Extract trace and that Minian unit's C. Its median is .82.

| defn         | median | pass ≥ 5 | AUC (Minian) | ρ (Minian trace r) |
| ------------ | ------ | -------- | ------------ | ------------------ |
| a_disc       | 13.9   | 744      | .640         | .350               |
| a_ext        | 14.4   | 752      | .621         | .326               |
| **b_full**   | 31.2   | 814      | **.800**     | **.749**           |
| c_signed     | 27.5   | 813      | .797         | .733               |
| c_signed_win | 12.9   | 704      | .625         | .320               |
| c_signed_lab | 27.5   | 813      | .797         | .734               |
| c_model      | 34.6   | 814      | .796         | .726               |

- **Flips from `a_disc` to `b_full`:** 70 fail → pass and 0 pass → fail. The signed or lab-convention variants give 70 / 1.
- **Clean failures.** 309 cells agree with Minian (footprint r ≥ .5) and have trace r ≥ .8. Of those, 19 fail on the window and 0 fail on any whole-session definition. These are the PI's "clean but failing" cells.
- **σ ratio.** The zero-skip σ over the signed σ has a median of 0.87 (p10 .77, p90 .96), and its ρ with the zero fraction is −.70. The more of the time a cell sits at the clip, the smaller its zero-skip σ, not the larger.

## Real data cross-check: the PI's Joint run

The run is Samara's data: 2 sessions, Joint, 393 cells, 29.8 fps. The source is the exported `cala-export.npz`, read only. The coordinator found this; the numbers were reproduced for this note.

| session  | exported `qc_peak_snr` | last 1200 frames | last 60 s (1787 frames) | whole session | whole session, MAD of all Δ |
| -------- | ---------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------- | ------------- | --------------------------- |
| 14_30_33 | 5.3 / .53              | 8.0 / .78        | 8.5 / .79               | 17.4 / .98    | 25.1 / .99                  |
| 14_44_22 | 5.3 / .53              | 5.3 / .55        | 6.0 / .65               | 18.3 / .99    | 27.1 / 1.00                 |

Each entry is median / pass fraction at 5.

- **Shared QC.** Both exports carry identical QC: the last minute of Discover, which is the end of session 2. Recomputed on the session's own last 60 s at the real fps (this PR's default), session 1 passes 79 % instead of 53 %. On the whole session, 98–99 % pass. Fail → pass from the exported values to whole session: 176 and 180 cells.
- **The all-pairs column.** The MAD over all differences, zero pairs included, reads higher still. The long clipped stretches give zero differences that pull the MAD down, which is why `stats.rs` skips them. It is not a symmetric estimate on clipped traces. The signed traces give the symmetric one.

## The app path

| stage    | QC monitor                                          | what the Cell QC tab and the export showed                                                                    |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Discover | one monitor over the pass, `fps` from the recording | snapshot every 1 s: last 60 s of Discover traces                                                              |
| Refine   | none (`refine-store.ts`, `qc: false`)               | unchanged: the last Discover snapshot. Ids can be stale after Refine merges.                                  |
| Extract  | none (`pass2-store.ts`, `qc: false`)                | unchanged: the last Discover snapshot. This was also exported as `qc_peak_snr` / `qc_pass` for every session. |

With this PR, after Extract (`App.tsx` → `extract-qc.ts`):

- The tab shows the QC of the extracted traces over the recording.
- Each session's export carries the QC of its own frames (`multi-run.ts` → `sessionQc`).
- Demixing, neighbour and motion metrics stay those of Discover. The duplicate flag is kept, and pass is recomputed.
- Cells Extract did not solve are dropped.
- The statistic is Rust `QcConfig::trace_stats`, the same function the monitor calls, through the WASM binding `QcMonitor.spanStats`.

## Frame rate

`QcConfig::for_fps(fps)` converts every QC duration (`qc::durations`) at the recording's rate. `Default` is `for_fps(20)`, identical to the old frame counts, which a test asserts.

| setting             | duration | frames at 20 Hz |
| ------------------- | -------- | --------------- |
| window              | 60 s     | 1200            |
| refresh             | 5 s      | 100             |
| min history         | 5 s      | 100             |
| residual τ          | 30 s     | 600             |
| churn window        | 60 s     | 1200            |
| motion burn-in      | 15 s     | 300             |
| motion block        | 25 s     | 500             |
| motion minimum fold | 5 s      | 100             |

`residual_every` (8) is a compute cadence and stays a count. `slow_window_s` and `smooth_s` were already seconds.

The app passes the rate from `FrameSourceInfo.fps`:

- **Files:** timestamps, then metaData.json, never the AVI header.
- **Live:** the hello message.
- **Joint:** the first session's rate, with a warning when the sessions differ; each session's export QC uses that session's own rate.

With no rate (`fpsSource: 'none'`), the fallback is no longer silent:

- The recording's warning says "time-based settings ... assume 20 Hz".
- The Recording panel shows "unknown (assuming 20 Hz)".
- The fast loop logs a console warning.

## Signed traces

The signed-trace work (worktree `CaLab-wt-signed`, uncommitted at the time) supplied `dump_signed.f32` through its `qc_eval` build. This PR does **not** depend on it.

- On these data the signed σ does not improve the ranking over the current σ on the same span. It does make σ symmetric and well defined for silent cells, with no zero-pair rule needed.
- To adopt the lab convention exactly (`measured_snr`: MAD over all pairs, detrended top 1 %) on the whole session, it needs the signed traces. With the signed σ: AUC .950 / .916 / .797 and ρ(GT SNR) .888 / .908.
- The signed traces read about 14 % lower than `b_full`, because the clipped σ is biased low.
